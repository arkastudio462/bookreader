## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, gallery-white atmosphere tailored for digital literature curation and high-focus reading experiences. Conceived under the principles of deliberate minimalism and editorial discipline, the canvas steps entirely into the background—acting as neutral museum framing that yields maximum visual presence to book covers, editorial collections, and publication metadata.

The aesthetic fuses modern Swiss clarity with functional gallery framing:
- **Tone & Mood:** Disciplined, intellectual, quiet, and definitive. It replaces loud gradients and ornamental decoration with strict tonal contrast, immaculate proportions, and structural breathing room.
- **Audience:** Discerning readers, publishers, literary curators, and students who demand swift, distraction-free discovery and deep reading clarity.
- **Visual Metaphor:** The white gallery wall (`#ffffff`) met by uncompromising ink (`#141414`), nuanced by subtle paper-like wash fills (`#f3f3f3`, `#f0f0f0`) and activated exclusively by an electric blue pulse (`#0066ff`) for verified commercial value, purchases, and active selections.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an austere, near-monochrome tonal scale punctuated by a single high-voltage commercial hue. Depth, priority, and surface divisions are communicated through precise tint variations rather than heavy drop shadows.

### The Monochrome Palette
- **Canvas (`#ffffff`):** The primary light-mode background. Serves as base zero for pages, primary cards, modals, and drawers.
- **Canvas-Soft (`#f3f3f3`):** Neutral wash used for recessed background containers, secondary card highlights, active segment backing, and alternating data rows.
- **Field & Hairline-Soft (`#f0f0f0`):** Subtle neutral fill for form inputs in their resting state, as well as the default hairline boundary for white cards floating against white canvas.
- **Hairline (`#e0e0e0`):** Structured 1px divider for structural seams, table cell borders, and outlined interactive elements.
- **Ink (`#141414`):** The definitive near-black ink. Applied to primary headings, core body copy, active CTA button fills, and the monolithic high-contrast footer.
- **Ink-Soft (`#262626`) & Text-Muted (`#707070`):** Secondary editorial levels for subtitles, book authors, categories, and supporting metadata.
- **Text-Faint (`#adadad`):** Tertiary level reserved exclusively for placeholder values, fine print, and inactive indicators.

### The Chromatic Accent
- **Electric Blue (`#0066ff`):** Strictly governed and never overused. It signifies high-value conversion elements: promo badges, subscription discount indicators, active commercial transactions, and verified bookstore indicators.

## Typography

Typography acts as the structural armature of the layout. Inter serves as the precise, unembellished type engine, balanced through extreme weight contrast and zero tracking:
- **Headlines:** Set in compact, tight vertical leading (ratio 1.0 to 1.13) with a medium-bold weight (600) to render declarative statements with architectural impact. Major editorial headlines end with a clean full stop.
- **Subtitles & Lead Paragraphs:** Rendered in light weight (300) at 20px, producing a delicate, high-end editorial feel that counterbalances heavy headline statements.
- **Body Text:** Grounded at regular weight (400) with a 1.38 line height to provide maximum legibility across dense literary abstracts, catalog reviews, and pricing breakdowns.
- **Punctuation & Letter-Spacing:** Strictly `0px` letter spacing across all scales to retain the natural, mechanical cadence of modern digital typesetting.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial rhythm relies on a base 8px increment (with a 4px micro-rhythm) to maintain strict vertical and horizontal alignment across screen sizes.

### Responsive Grids & Margins
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** Max container width capped at 1440px. 12-column fluid grid, 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`), and 48px canvas margins (`margin-lg`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters (`gutter-md`) and 32px outer canvas margin (`margin-md`). Book catalogs shift into 3-up or 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters (`gutter`) and 16px margins (`margin`). Hero display typography steps down to ensure zero awkward wrapping.

### Section Breathing Room
Editorial sections are separated by expansive vertical gaps (`80px` on standard modules, `120px` between major catalog explorations and hero transitions). This generous breathing room prevents visual clutter and lets readers evaluate each curated collection individually.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely without traditional drop shadows. Depth is communicated via pure tonal surface nesting and 1px hairline perimeters.

### The Tiered Elevation System
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure white (`#ffffff`). The floor of all content views.
- **Level 1 (Recessed Wash):** Subtle `#f3f3f3` fill with no boundary border. Applied to feature sections, background trays, and non-active segmented controls.
- **Level 2 (Framed Island):** Pure white surface framed by a 1px `#f0f0f0` hairline edge (`hairline-soft`). Applied to book overview cards, editorial spotlights, and review containers.
- **Level 3 (Focused & Active Seams):** 1px `#e0e0e0` hairline borders for table cells, outlined buttons, and cart drawers.
- **Focused State:** Direct transition from Level 1/2 to a crisp, 2px solid `#141414` ink boundary around interactive fields.
- **Monolithic Inversion:** The base footer and select critical banners flip polarity to solid `#141414` ink with white content, acting as visual anchors at the conclusion of the page.

## Shapes

The design system employs a strict geometric dualism: stadium pills for interactive units, generous curves for content containers, and subtle squircles for brand assets.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Stadium Full Pill (`roundedness: 3`, `rounded-full` / `9999px`):** The signature shape for all interactive click targets, including action buttons, filter tags, floating navigation bars, badge overlays, and search pills.
- **Container Curvature (24px / `rounded-md`):** Applied to book presentation cards, modal frames, and the top corners of the dark footer.
- **Media & Field Curvature (16px / `rounded-sm`):** Applied to form fields, nested tiles, and preview elements.
- **Asset Squircles (30% Radius):** Standardized 30% continuous squircle radius for publisher brand marks, app tiles, and software integration icons.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Stadium full pill (`rounded-full`), filled with `#141414` ink with white text. Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px (`space-lg`), typography `action`. Hover state dims opacity to 90%.
- **Outline Button:** Stadium pill with a transparent canvas fill, framed by a 1px solid `#e0e0e0` border. Text in `#141414`.
- **Soft Pill Button:** Stadium pill filled with `#f3f3f3` with `#141414` text, providing a muted secondary action.
- **Commercial Action:** Reserved for final cart checkouts or primary subscriptions; utilizes `#0066ff` background with crisp white typography.

### Floating Navigation Pill
- A detached, horizontally centered stadium pill (`rounded-full`) anchored above the viewport.
- Surface: `#ffffff` canvas with a 1px `#f0f0f0` border, containing navigational links in `action` typography (`#141414` for active, `#707070` for inactive).

### Badges & Chips
- **Commercial Badge:** Solid `#0066ff` pill with pure white text, set in `badge-label`. Used for pricing discounts and exclusive literary releases.
- **Translucent Scrim Badge:** `rgba(115, 115, 115, 0.56)` backdrop pill overlaid directly onto book cover imagery for reading time, chapter counts, or audio badges.

### Input Fields
- **Resting State:** Height 48px, 16px corner radius (`rounded-sm`), filled with `#f0f0f0` (`colors.field`), borderless. Typography `body-md` in `#141414` with placeholder text in `#adadad`.
- **Focused State:** Retains the `#f0f0f0` field fill while gaining an immediate 2px solid `#141414` ink ring. Zero blur shadows.

### Cards & Media Tiles
- **Book Container Card:** 24px corner radius (`rounded-md`), white canvas background, bounded by a 1px `#f0f0f0` border. Padding 24px.
- **Media Tiles:** Book cover art is framed with clean 16px corner radii, maintaining consistent aspect ratios without heavy external drop shadows.

### Inverted Monolithic Footer
- Set in `#141414` ink with top corners rounded to 24px (`rounded-md`).
- Text links rendered in `#adadad` shifting to white on hover, anchored by declarative white heading statements.
